# Movement-practice systems: building blocks, progression, dosing, session structure, integration with strength training

> **How these notes were gathered.** Every official domain (idoportal.com, animalflow.com, gmb.io, originalstrength.net, movnat.com) failed DNS resolution from this cloud session, so I could not open and read any page in full. Every finding below comes from **web-search result snippets and summaries**. Search returned many official URLs, so a lot of the content is from first-party pages, but I could only see excerpts of them. Labels used: **[Official]** = the snippet came from the system's own domain or its own manual; **[Secondhand]** = coach blog, media, forum or aggregator. A report writer should treat exact numbers as "reported in a snippet of X", not as verified against the full page. ## 1. Core components and named movement families for each system

### Takeaway
All six systems break movement into a small set of named families. Animal Flow has 6 components built on 4 base positions. GMB Elements has 4 quadrupedal patterns. Original Strength has the "Big Five" resets. MovNat has 3 skill categories (locomotive, manipulative, combative). Ido Portal's Movement Culture is organised around challenges (squat, hanging) plus domains (locomotion, floreio, hand balancing). Ginastica Natural is ground locomotion drawn from jiu-jitsu and yoga. For an app, the shared skeleton is: joint prep (wrists, breathing), static holds or positions, travelling patterns, transitions, and freeform combinations or "flow".

### Cited Findings

**Animal Flow (Mike Fitch)**
- [Official] The official site and the Level 1 manual name six components: (1) Wrist Mobilizations, (2) Activations, (3) Form-Specific Stretches, (4) Traveling Forms, (5) Switches and Transitions, (6) Flows. Flows are where the other components come together. — [Animal Flow: Understanding the 6 Components](https://animalflow.com/flowist_post/understanding-the-6-components-of-animal-flow/); [Animal Flow: The Six Components](https://animalflow.com/?p=50635); [Animal Flow Level 1 Manual 2023 (PDF)](https://animalflow.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Animal-Flow-Level-1-Manual-English-2023.pdf)
- [Official] Wrist mobilizations are simple movements that prepare the hands and wrists. Animal Flow encourages doing them before, during and after any practice. — [Animal Flow: 6 Components](https://animalflow.com/flowist_post/understanding-the-6-components-of-animal-flow/)
- [Official] Activations are isometric holds in two positions, **Beast** and **Crab**, which are two of the system's four base positions. Activations are progressed by reducing the number of points of contact with the ground. — [Animal Flow: 6 Components](https://animalflow.com/flowist_post/understanding-the-6-components-of-animal-flow/)
- [Official] The Level 1 manual's contents list these form-specific stretches: Loaded Beast → Unload, Wave Unload, Ape Reach, Crab Reach, Scorpion Reach, Beast Reach, and a Form-Specific Stretch Flow. — [Animal Flow Level 1 Manual 2023 (PDF)](https://animalflow.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Animal-Flow-Level-1-Manual-English-2023.pdf)
- [Secondhand] Form-specific stretches start in an animal form and then move through a wide range of motion. — summary returned with [What Is Animal Flow (bioedge)](https://bioedge-cms-crm.vercel.app/what-is/animal-flow)
- [Secondhand] Some secondary sources say Animal Flow has 5 components rather than 6, which conflicts with the official 6. — [Ultrahuman blog](https://www.ultrahuman.com/blog/animal-flow-getting-back-to-your-roots/); contradicted by [Animal Flow official](https://animalflow.com/?p=50635)
- Not confirmed in the snippets I could see: the travelling forms Beast, Ape and Crab named in the brief, and the switches and transitions (Underswitch, Side Kick-Through, Scorpion Reach). A page titled "Mike Fitch, Reaching Underswitch" appears on the official site. — [Animal Flow](https://animalflow.com/?p=240095)

**GMB Fitness: Elements**
- [Official] Elements is built on four quadrupedal patterns: **Bear, Monkey, Frogger, Crab**. Many variations of each keep you on all fours while moving through space. It is described as "four movements, 20 minutes a day, no equipment, structured progressions", delivered through the GMB app with daily sessions. — [GMB Elements](https://gmb.io/e/); [GMB Elements vs Animal Flow](https://gmb.io/animal-flow-elements/)
- [Official] What each pattern is for, in GMB's words. Bear: inverted on the hands with hips high, building shoulder and upper-back strength while decompressing the spine. Monkey: a deep lateral move that opens the hips through repeated squat transitions. Frogger: a forward weight shift from a squat onto the hands, which deepens the squat and builds arm, shoulder and core stability. Crab: the supine counterpart, though I saw no specific description in the snippets. — [GMB Locomotion](https://gmb.io/locomotion/); [GMB Resilience Locomotion](https://gmb.io/resilience-locomotion/)
- [Official] GMB says high Monkey and high Frogger variations carry over to handstand balance. The support is a client testimonial, so it is anecdotal. — [GMB Locomotion for Handstand](https://gmb.io/locomotion-handstand/)

**Original Strength (Tim Anderson, Geoff Neupert)**
- [Official] The "Big Five" resets are diaphragmatic breathing, head control (head nods), rolling, rocking, and crawling (gait). They are the foundation of the "Pressing RESET" method. — [Original Strength: Pressing RESET, a tailored approach](https://mail.originalstrength.net/blog/2026/03/24/pressing-reset-a-tailored-approach-to-movement-and-vitality); [OS: 5 resets you might not know are resets](https://originalstrength.net/blog/the-top-5-resets-that-did-not-make-the-big-5)
- [Official] OS presents these as movements people are born with, which act as a "reset button" for a nervous system made "glitchy" by stress, injury or sedentary habits. These are the company's claims. I found no peer-reviewed support. — [OS blog: Unlocking your best health](https://originalstrength.net/blog/2026/01/02/unlocking-your-best-health-the-power-of-your-bodys-natural-design)
- [Official] OS also offers crawling specifically to lifters (leopard crawl) and runs a 30-day crawling challenge. — [OS: Why weightlifters should be leopard crawling](https://mail.originalstrength.net/blog/tags/pressing+RESET); [30-Day Crawling Challenge (Scribd copy)](https://www.scribd.com/document/342366801/30daycrawlingchallenge-pdf)

**MovNat (Erwan Le Corre)**
- [Official] Skills fall into three categories. **Locomotive**: ground movements, balancing, walking, running, jumping, climbing, aquatic. **Manipulative**: lifting, carrying, throwing, catching. **Combative**: grappling, striking. — [What Is MovNat](https://movnat.com/what-is-movnat/)
- [Official] MovNat aims to give people movement skills, physiological preparedness and mindset for "practical, adaptable performance". — [What Is MovNat](https://movnat.com/what-is-movnat/)
- [Official] Beginner combos include crawling, hanging, jumping, balancing and get-ups, each with regressions and progressions. — [MovNat: Beginner Combo Workout Vol. 1](https://movnat.com/beginner-movnat-combo-workout-vol-1/)

**Ido Portal: Movement Culture**
- [Official, excerpt only] The blog hosts the Hanging Challenge, plus follow-up posts on one-arm hangs, "more hang" and "integrated hang". It mentions that the squat challenge went viral and has "over 13,000 active members". — [Ido Portal blog: Hanging](https://idoportal.com/blog/hanging/); [Ido Portal: One-arm hang](https://idoportal.com/blog/onearmhang/); [Ido Portal: More hang](https://idoportal.com/blog/morehang/); [Ido Portal: Integrated hang](https://idoportal.com/blog/integhang/)
- [Secondhand] Portal's background is modern dance, acrobatics and capoeira. His method mixes gymnastic skills, hand balancing, stretching and capoeira. — [FloGrappling](https://www.flograppling.com/articles/5049816-movement-training-in-jiu-jitsu-mma-mcgregor-and-ido-portal-vs-diaz); [RollItUp forum](https://www.rollitup.org/t/calisthenics-ido-portal-method-and-ginastica-natural-thread.855941/)
- [Official, show notes] In Huberman Lab Ep. 77 (20 June 2022), Portal discusses reflexive versus deliberate movement and "Entry Points to Movement". A summary of the episode lists the squat challenge as "accumulate 30 minutes in squat position per day". — [Huberman Lab: Ido Portal](https://www.hubermanlab.com/episode/ido-portal-the-science-and-practice-of-movement); [Podcast Notes summary](https://podcastnotes.org/huberman-lab/episode-77-ido-portal-the-science-practice-of-movement-huberman-lab/)

**Ginastica Natural (Alvaro Romano) and capoeira-derived ground movement**
- [Official/vendor] Ginastica Natural was created by Alvaro Romano, a BJJ practitioner. It combines hatha-yoga stretching, flexibility and breathing with jiu-jitsu ground movements and "natural movements of the human body". Its positions carry animal names only as illustration. — [Ginastica Natural store](https://ginasticanatural.bigcartel.com/product/ginastica-natural-by-alvaro-romano); [BJJ Fanatics](https://bjjfanatics.com/products/ginastica-natural-by-alvaro-romano)
- [Secondhand, 2026 guide] Its foundational sequences are described as ground-based locomotion patterns that build spinal articulation, hip mobility and the ability to move fluidly through irregular positions. — [LetsBJJ guide](https://letsbjj.com/en/news/news-20260623-guide-alvaro-romano-ginastica-natural?lang=en)

### Inferences
- A cross-system taxonomy for the app could be: **Prep** (wrists, breathing, head and neck), **Static positions/holds** (Beast, Crab, deep squat, hangs), **Travelling patterns** (Beast/Bear, Ape/Monkey, Crab, Frogger, crawl, roll), **Transitions** (switches, get-ups, rolls), and **Combinations** (flows, MovNat combos). Animal Flow's 6 components map onto this almost one-to-one, so it is the cleanest template.
- GMB's Bear, Monkey, Frogger and Crab map closely onto Animal Flow's Beast, Ape, Frogger-like jumps and Crab. An app could use generic names to avoid trademarked naming.

### Gaps
- I could not open the full official pages, so I could not confirm the complete lists of Animal Flow travelling forms and switches, the content of Ido Portal's locomotion and floreio curricula, or GMB's full Elements variation tree.
- I found no source on "Primal Movement Chains". No search result defined it.
- I found no source covering capoeira floreio as a structured progression, or the curriculum of Ido Portal's locomotion and hand-balancing work.

## 2. Progression levels and criteria

### Takeaway
Most systems progress by **quality and automaticity, not fixed numbers**. GMB moves you up when a skill is automatic and back a level if it breaks down. Animal Flow progresses activations by removing points of contact, and has formal certification levels with rubric criteria. MovNat and Animal Flow have tiered coach certifications (L1/L2/L3), but these are not consumer progression ladders. I found no published hold-time thresholds such as "hold Beast X seconds before travelling".

### Cited Findings
- [Official] GMB moves up a skill level once the skill becomes automatic, and drops back one level if it breaks down. — [GMB feed / method](https://gmb.io/feed/)
- [Official] Animal Flow activations are made harder by reducing points of contact, for example lifting a hand or a foot from static Beast or Crab. — [Animal Flow: 6 Components](https://animalflow.com/flowist_post/understanding-the-6-components-of-animal-flow/)
- [Official] Animal Flow's instructor test-out rubric sets quality criteria per movement. Examples: knees "remain flexed, 1 inch from the ground, toes tucked under" in loaded Beast, and "knees maintain 1 inch from the ground throughout" in the Crab movement. — rubric content returned in search with [Animal Flow](https://animalflow.com/?p=361478) and the [Level 1 Manual](https://animalflow.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Animal-Flow-Level-1-Manual-English-2023.pdf)
- [Official] Animal Flow runs Level 1 and Level 2 certifications and a Master Instructor track. — [Animal Flow Level 1 Manual](https://animalflow.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Animal-Flow-Level-1-Manual-English-2023.pdf); [AF Master Instructor Manual](https://animalflow.com/mihome/mi_level1videos/af-master-instructor-manual-complete-2017-2)
- [Official] MovNat certifies coaches at Level 1, 2 and 3. Level 1 "is all about the basics". Level 2 requires a current L1 and three evaluations: written, physical competence and coaching assessment. — [MovNat Level 1](https://www.movnat.com/level-1-certification/); [MovNat Level 2](https://movnat.com/event/level-2-certification-san-diego-ca-2/)
- [Secondhand] The MovNat L1 exam involves studying a roughly 160-page manual, then written and physical exams at the end of 3 days. — [Kat's Dynamic Bodywork](http://www.katsdynamicbodywork.com/index.php/intro-to-movnat/)
- [Official] MovNat beginner movements come with regressions and progressions. Sets, reps, time, height, distance and weight are "suggestions" to be modified to individual needs. — [MovNat Beginner Combo Vol. 1](https://movnat.com/beginner-movnat-combo-workout-vol-1/); [MovNat: 3 Natural Movement Workouts for Beginners](https://movnat.com/3-natural-movement-workouts-for-beginners/)
- [Secondhand] Ido Portal's 30/30 squat: a reviewer reports the purist view that the challenge only counts once you have totalled 30 full days of 30 minutes. This is not an official rule. — [Straight Talking Fitness review](https://straighttalkingfitness.com/2022/01/20/my-experience-with-ido-portals-30-30-squat-challenge-a-review/)
- [Secondhand] Ido Portal's hanging progressions run from two-arm to one-arm hangs (blog post "onearmhang") and "integrated" hanging, which indicates a passive → active → dynamic → one-arm direction. — [Ido Portal: One-arm hang](https://idoportal.com/blog/onearmhang/); [Ido Portal: Integrated hang](https://idoportal.com/blog/integhang/)

### Inferences
- A defensible model for the app is **criteria-gated progression**: unlock the next variation when the quality checklist is met (knee height, hip position, smooth breathing) across N sessions. Add GMB-style auto-regression: step back a level if quality fails. This matches how the official sources describe progression better than fixed hold-time gates do.
- Where the app needs numbers, MovNat's "suggestions, adjust to you" framing supports user-adjustable defaults.

### Gaps
- I found no official hold-time or distance thresholds, for example "hold Beast 30 s before travelling" or "crawl 20 m before progressing".
- I could not see the definitions of passive, active and dynamic hangs in any snippet.
- I found no GMB level count or weekly progression schedule for Elements.

## 3. Concrete dosing (time, distance, reps, frequency)

### Takeaway
The most concrete numbers come from Ido Portal's challenges (30 min a day of accumulated squatting for 30 days, and 7 min a day of accumulated hanging for 30 days, split into 1–3 sessions) and Original Strength (about 2 minutes per reset, around 10 minutes for all five, or a 3-minute "daily" minimum). GMB offers 15, 30 or 45-minute sessions, with "20 minutes a day" quoted for Elements. MovNat sessions run 3–5×/week, with a combo of 3–5 rounds kept under 20 minutes and about 15 minutes per emphasised skill.

### Cited Findings

**Ido Portal**
- [Official, excerpt] Hanging Challenge: "7 min of accumulative time (not in one go but spread throughout the day) performing various types of hangs - passive/active/dynamic" for 30 consecutive days. Later posts allow the total to be split into 1–3 sessions. A 2014 post says the numbers were chosen as attainable but still effective. — [Ido Portal blog: Hanging](https://idoportal.com/blog/hanging/); [Antranik: The Hanging Month](https://antranik.org/the-hanging-month/)
- [Secondhand, consistent across several sources] 30/30 Squat Challenge: accumulate 30 minutes a day in a deep squat for 30 days. Time need not be continuous and can be spread across the day in short bursts. — [Ignore Limits](https://ignorelimits.com/ido-portal-3030-squat-challenge/); [Evolifting 2014](https://evolifting.wordpress.com/2014/02/12/ido-portals-3030-squat-challenge/); [Place of Persistence](http://placeofpersistence.com/30-30-squat-challenge-by-ido-portal/); [Podcast Notes, Huberman Ep. 77](https://podcastnotes.org/huberman-lab/episode-77-ido-portal-the-science-practice-of-movement-huberman-lab/)
- [Secondhand] 30/30 guidelines: no rest days in the 30 days. A relaxed or rounded spine is fine because the squat is treated as a rest position. Stand up and move if knee or hip pain appears. Use a small heel raise if heels cannot stay down. — [Ignore Limits](https://ignorelimits.com/ido-portal-3030-squat-challenge/)
- [Secondhand] Ramp-up suggestion: build over about a week, 5 → 10 → 20 → 30 minutes. This comes from a coach blog, not Portal. — [Ignore Limits](https://ignorelimits.com/ido-portal-3030-squat-challenge/)
- [Secondhand, anecdotal] Participants report their hips feel better afterwards. — [StrongFirst forum: 30/30 Squat Challenge](https://www.strongfirst.com/community/threads/30-30-squat-challenge.2989/)

**Original Strength**
- [Interview with founder] Five main resets at about 2 minutes each, roughly 10 minutes total. — [Legion Athletics / Muscle for Life Ep. 733, Tim Anderson](https://legionathletics.com/muscle-for-life-episode-733-tim-anderson-interview/)
- [Official, older guide] A "three minute reset" is easy to do daily. — [Pressing Reset guidebook PDF, Tim Anderson](http://forzafit.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/05/The-New-Pressing-Reset.pdf)
- [Official blog] One routine uses about 1 minute per movement plus 5 minutes of diaphragmatic breathing. Another post describes about 15 minutes of gentle movement. — [OS: Simple Daily Reset Restoration Plan](https://originalstrength.net/blog/simple-daily-reset-restoration-plan/); [OS: RESETS + Easy Strength](https://originalstrength.net/blog/2021/01/11/do-this-to-be-the-best-you/)
- [Secondhand, forum] A long-time practitioner says to do each reset until you feel looser rather than counting reps. Another user does about 2 minutes per exercise as a warm-up or active recovery. — [StrongFirst forum](https://www.strongfirst.com/community/threads/original-strength-im-a-believer.21130/post-389603)
- [Official] OS runs a 30-day crawling challenge format. — [30-Day Crawling Challenge](https://www.scribd.com/document/342366801/30daycrawlingchallenge-pdf)

**GMB**
- [Official] Elements: "four movements, 20 minutes a day, no equipment". — [GMB Elements vs Animal Flow](https://gmb.io/animal-flow-elements/)
- [Official] In the GMB app, users choose 15, 30 or 45-minute sessions. 15-minute sessions can serve as a warm-up, cool-down or rest-day session. — [GMB FAQ](https://gmb.io/faq/)

**Animal Flow**
- [Secondhand] BarBend's beginner AF mobility routine: each movement for 40 seconds, 3–4 rounds. — [BarBend: Animal Flow Mobility](https://barbend.com/animal-flow-mobility/)
- [Official/secondhand] There is a 30-minute beginner class led by Mike Fitch. Tom's Guide reviewers tested 20-minute and 30-minute daily routines. — [Tom's Guide 20-min](https://www.tomsguide.com/wellness/fitness/forget-weights-this-20-minute-animal-flow-routine-builds-full-body-functional-strength-using-just-your-body-weight); [Tom's Guide 30-min daily for a week](https://www.tomsguide.com/features/i-tried-a-30-minute-animal-flow-exercise-routine-every-day-for-a-week-heres-my-results)

**MovNat**
- [Official] The beginner guide suggests the workouts 3–5×/week for one month. The beginner e-book offers 20 weeks at 3 sessions a week. — [MovNat Beginner's Guide](https://movnat.com/beginners-guide-movnat/); [MovNat Beginner E-Book](https://movnat.com/store/movnat-beginner-workouts-e-book/)
- [Official] About 15 minutes per emphasised skill, 1–2 skills per session. The combo circuit runs at least 3 rounds; Vol. 1 says 3–5 rounds with minimal rest, total time under 20 minutes. — [MovNat: How I Program a Session](https://movnat.com/how-i-program-a-natural-movement-fitness-session/); [MovNat Beginner Combo Vol. 1](https://movnat.com/beginner-movnat-combo-workout-vol-1/)

### Inferences
- Two dosing models suit an app. **Accumulation challenges** (Portal) are daily minute totals spread across the day, which fit "movement snacks" with a progress ring. **Bounded sessions** (GMB, MovNat, AF) last 10–45 minutes.
- Sensible defaults from the sources: a daily prep or reset of 3–10 minutes (OS), a movement skill block of 15–20 minutes (GMB, MovNat), standalone sessions of 30–45 minutes, and accumulators of 7 min hang and up to 30 min squat, ramped in over a week or more.

### Gaps
- I found no official Animal Flow warm-up duration, and no Mike Fitch statement on minutes of AF before lifting.
- I found no official distance targets for crawling or travelling forms.

## 4. Integration with lifting and running (placement and weekly frequency)

### Takeaway
Every system positions itself as compatible with strength training, mostly as **pre-session prep** (OS resets, AF activations and wrist mobs, GMB "Prepare"). The second most common role is **short rest-day or recovery sessions** (GMB 15-min sessions, OS resets as active recovery). MovNat and GMB also run as **standalone skill sessions** 3–5×/week. Portal's challenges are **daily accumulations** outside formal sessions. I found no source giving specific guidance on movement practice relative to running.

### Cited Findings
- [Official] Animal Flow suggests "using some Animal Flow Activations at the start of any training" to shift attention to breathing and body awareness, and calls it a way to "prepare for the physical outcomes of your training". — [Animal Flow](https://animalflow.com/?p=339574); [Animal Flow: Integrated Movement](https://animalflow.com/flowist_post/integrated-movement/)
- [Official] Wrist mobilizations before, during and after any practice. — [Animal Flow: 6 Components](https://animalflow.com/flowist_post/understanding-the-6-components-of-animal-flow/)
- [Secondhand] BarBend argues AF is a good core of a lifter's warm-up because, done correctly, the movements are hard to do on autopilot. — [BarBend](https://barbend.com/?p=98480)
- [Official] GMB's session template is the **5P protocol**: Prepare (a warm-up specific to the session's main work), Practice (skill work), Play (exploring the skills at a comfortable level), Push (harder work), Ponder (reflect and plan the next session). — [GMB Method](https://gmb.io/method/); [GMB feed](https://gmb.io/feed/)
- [Official] GMB has an article on combining movement training with kettlebells, and positions 15-minute sessions as warm-up, cool-down or rest-day work. — [GMB: Integrating Movement and Kettlebell Training](https://gmb.io/kettlebells/); [GMB FAQ](https://gmb.io/faq/)
- [Official] Original Strength pairs resets with "Easy Strength". Tim Anderson often presses reset before training, and suggests resetting or warming up with preparation movements "before engaging in the training portion of your routine". — [OS: RESETS + Easy Strength](https://originalstrength.net/blog/2021/01/11/do-this-to-be-the-best-you/); [OS: Super Simple Strength](https://originalstrength.net/blog/2021/10/18/super-simple-stength/)
- [Official] OS explicitly pitches leopard crawling to weightlifters. — [OS: Why weightlifters should be leopard crawling](https://mail.originalstrength.net/blog/tags/pressing+RESET)
- [Secondhand, forum] Users do OS resets as a warm-up or as active recovery. One user did them daily for about 4 weeks and noticed no benefit. Another stopped rocking because of knee irritation. — [StrongFirst forum](https://www.strongfirst.com/community/goto/post?id=389551)
- [Official] A MovNat session runs warm-up → emphasis (1–2 skills, about 15 min each) → combo (circuit, 3+ rounds) → cool-down, at 3–5×/week for beginners. MovNat also has articles on training in urban and gym environments. — [MovNat: How I Program a Session](https://movnat.com/how-i-program-a-natural-movement-fitness-session/); [MovNat Beginner's Guide](https://movnat.com/beginners-guide-movnat/); [MovNat: Urban & Gym Training](https://movnat.com/dannys-urban-training-journal-1/)
- [Official/secondhand] Ido Portal's challenges are daily, accumulated across the day (hang: 1–3 sessions), with no rest days in the 30/30. — [Ido Portal: Hanging](https://idoportal.com/blog/hanging/); [Ignore Limits](https://ignorelimits.com/ido-portal-3030-squat-challenge/)

### Inferences
- Placement options with source backing:
  - **Warm-up / prep** before lifting: OS resets (3–10 min), AF wrist mobs plus Beast/Crab activations, GMB "Prepare".
  - **Dedicated skill block** inside a session or on its own day: GMB Practice/Play, MovNat emphasis plus combo.
  - **Finisher / conditioning**: MovNat combo circuits (3–5 rounds, under 20 min), GMB "Push".
  - **Rest-day / active recovery**: GMB 15-min sessions, OS resets.
  - **Daily snacks**: Portal's squat and hang accumulation, OS's "3-minute daily reset".
- A reasonable weekly default: daily 3–10-minute prep or snacks, plus 2–3 dedicated 15–30-minute skill sessions. This sits inside MovNat's 3–5×/week and GMB's daily-or-flexible framing. This is my synthesis, not a prescription from any one source.
- Running: none of the sources gave running-specific placement. By extension from the warm-up guidance, short prep before a run and longer sessions on easy or rest days would fit, but this is unsourced.

### Gaps
- I found no source on placing movement practice relative to running, such as before runs or away from hard run days.
- I found no explicit guidance on interference with heavy lower-body days, for example whether 30 minutes of deep squatting a day affects squat sessions.
- I found no official Animal Flow weekly frequency recommendation.

## 5. Coaching cues and quality criteria

### Takeaway
The most concrete published cues are Animal Flow's: knees about 1 inch off the ground in Beast and Crab, shoulders over wrists in the setup, shoulders shifting in front of the wrists in the unload, a neutral spine and a braced core. Portal's squat guidance is notably relaxed (a rounded spine is fine, heels may be propped). OS's main "cue" is to do each reset until you feel looser.

### Cited Findings
- [Official rubric] Loaded Beast end position: "knees remain flexed, 1 inch from the ground, toes tucked under". Crab travelling: "knees maintain 1 inch from the ground throughout". In the unload, the shoulders translate in front of the wrist line. — Animal Flow instructor rubric content returned with [Animal Flow](https://animalflow.com/?p=361478); [Level 1 Manual](https://animalflow.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Animal-Flow-Level-1-Manual-English-2023.pdf)
- [Secondhand] Lift the knees only high enough to slide a sheet of paper underneath. — [BarBend](https://barbend.com/animal-flow-mobility/)
- [Secondhand] Tabletop setup: shoulders stacked over wrists, hips over knees, brace, lift the knees about an inch. — [Tom's Guide](https://www.tomsguide.com/wellness/fitness/develop-lower-body-flexibility-and-build-ankle-strength-with-this-one-bodyweight-exercise)
- [Secondhand] Static Beast: retract the shoulder blades, keep the spine neutral, brace the core. — [Experience Life](https://experiencelife.lifetime.life/article/animal-flow-fitness/); [GoodRx](https://www.goodrx.com/well-being/movement-exercise/animal-flow-workout)
- [Secondhand] Wave Unload: shift weight forward until the shoulders are in front of the wrists. — [Experience Life](https://experiencelife.lifetime.life/article/animal-flow-fitness/)
- [Secondhand] Screen for wrist, shoulder or hip restrictions before adding ground-based loading. — [bioedge](https://bioedge-cms-crm.vercel.app/what-is/animal-flow)
- [Secondhand] 30/30 squat: no need to stay erect or neutral; the squat is a rest position. Heel support is fine. Stand and move if knees or hips hurt. — [Ignore Limits](https://ignorelimits.com/ido-portal-3030-squat-challenge/)
- [Secondhand, forum] OS: do each reset until you feel looser and move better, rather than to a fixed count. — [StrongFirst forum](https://www.strongfirst.com/community/threads/original-strength-im-a-believer.21130/post-389603)
- [Official] GMB's stated quality criterion for progressing is automaticity: progress when a movement is automatic, regress if it breaks down. — [GMB feed](https://gmb.io/feed/)

### Inferences
- An app checklist for quadrupedal work could be: hands under shoulders, knees under hips, knees about 1 inch off the floor (paper test), neutral spine with braced trunk, steady nasal or diaphragmatic breathing, quiet and controlled contacts. For progression, add "smooth and automatic".
- Wrist prep is the most universally recommended safety element and should be a default first step.

### Gaps
- I found no published cue sets for GMB's Monkey or Frogger, MovNat's crawling or get-ups, or Portal's locomotion or floreio. Those are likely inside paid programs or pages I could not open.
- I found no evidence base (studies) for any of these systems in the sources reviewed. Benefit claims, especially OS's nervous-system claims, are vendor claims.
